Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Aura Star ("Aura Star", "we", "us") is a social app built around a glowing 3D globe where you share moments and connect with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We designed Aura Star to be private by default.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo to create your account. You can add a display name, username, short bio, and country.
  • Content you create. Posts (title, text, photos), likes, comments, and the approximate place you choose to attach to a post.
  • Approximate location (optional). If you turn on location sharing, we store an approximate location (rounded, never your exact coordinates) so connected friends can see roughly where you are. This is opt-in and can be turned off at any time.
  • Friends & social graph. Friend connections you make, and per-friend preferences you set (nickname, avatar frame).
  • Basic technical data. Standard information needed to operate the service (e.g. app version, language, and logs for reliability and abuse prevention).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core features: showing posts on the globe, connecting friends, likes and comments.
  • To enforce your privacy choices (public vs. friends-only posts) and keep the community safe.
  • To improve reliability and prevent abuse, spam, and fraud.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. What others can see

  • Public posts are visible to anyone using Aura Star, shown at an approximate location.
  • Friends-only posts and shared location are visible only to friends you have connected with. This is enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the app.
  • We never reveal other users' email addresses — friend search by email never exposes the address itself.

4. Location, specifically

Location sharing is always optional. Coordinates are rounded to an approximate area (about a kilometre) before they are stored or shown, so your exact position is never displayed. You can stop sharing at any time in the app.

5. Service providers

We use trusted infrastructure providers to run Aura Star, including Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage) and Google (Sign-In). Map imagery is provided by Esri. These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the service.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can edit or delete your posts at any time. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below; we will remove your personal data except where we must retain it to comply with the law or prevent abuse.

7. Security

Access to data is controlled by row-level security rules so that people can only see what they are allowed to see. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

Aura Star is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

9. International users

Your information may be processed on servers located in other countries. By using Aura Star you consent to this processing, carried out in line with this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, provide a notice in the app.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy or a data request? Email us at support@aurastar.app.
