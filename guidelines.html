Community Guidelines

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Aura Star is a place to share real, positive moments and connect with friends across the world. A few simple rules keep it that way for everyone.

Be positive & respectful

Share genuine moments and treat people with kindness. No harassment, hate speech, threats, or bullying. This is not a place for competition, self-promotion spam, or putting others down.

Keep it safe

No illegal content, violence, or sexually explicit material. Don't post content that endangers or exploits anyone, especially minors.

Respect privacy

Don't post other people's private information or photos without consent. Never use location features to track, follow, or intimidate anyone. Remember that locations on Aura Star are intentionally approximate.

Be real

Be yourself. No impersonation, fake accounts, or deceptive content. Don't spam or manipulate likes, comments, or friend requests.

Reporting & enforcement

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, use the in-app report option. Our moderators review reports and may remove content or suspend accounts. Serious or repeated violations can lead to a permanent ban.

Thanks for helping keep Aura Star a bright, welcoming place. ✦
