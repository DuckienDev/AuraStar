Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Aura Star has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to keeping children safe on our platform and to complying with child safety laws everywhere we operate. This page sets out our standards against CSAE and how we enforce them.

What is prohibited

The following are strictly forbidden on Aura Star:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — any content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers a minor.
  • Grooming, sextortion, or any attempt to solicit, lure, or sexually contact a minor.
  • Trafficking, promoting, or facilitating the sexual exploitation of children in any form.
  • Accounts created by or impersonating minors in a sexualized way.

Minimum age

Aura Star is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum digital-consent age in your country, whichever is higher). Accounts we identify as belonging to underage users are removed.

How to report

Every moment and profile in the app can be reported with the in-app Report option (tap the ··· menu → Report). Reports of child safety concerns are prioritized. You can also email us directly at childsafety@aurastar.app — please include a link or username if you can.

How we respond

We review child safety reports promptly. When we confirm CSAE, we remove the content, permanently ban the accounts involved, preserve the relevant evidence, and report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the appropriate authorities as required by law.

Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and with the Google Play and Apple App Store child safety policies, including their standards against CSAE. We update these standards as our product and the law evolve.

Child safety point of contact

For any child safety matter, contact our designated point of contact at childsafety@aurastar.app.
