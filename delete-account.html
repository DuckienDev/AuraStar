Delete your account

Last updated: 6 November 2026

You can ask us to permanently delete your Aura Star account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This page explains what is removed and how to make the request.

Delete it inside the app

If you are signed in, the fastest way is: open Aura Star → Profile → Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Your account and data are removed right away.

Or request it here

If you can't open the app, enter the email address you signed up with and we'll delete the account for you. We may contact that address to verify the request before deleting.

What gets deleted

Your profile, your moments and photos, comments and reactions, friends and friend requests, approximate location data, and your sign-in credentials. Once deletion is complete this cannot be undone.

Timing & what we keep

Requests made in the app take effect immediately. Requests sent from this page are processed within 30 days. We may retain a limited amount of data for longer only where the law requires it (for example, to prevent fraud or abuse); that data is not used for any other purpose.

Need help?

You can also email us at support@aurastar.app and we'll handle it.
